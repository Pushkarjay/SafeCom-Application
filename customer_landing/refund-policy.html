Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective: May 15, 2026

Our Commitment: We aim to provide quality service. If we're unable to deliver, we ensure timely refunds.

1. Cancellation Terms

  • Free Cancellation: Up to 2 hours before scheduled service time
  • Late Cancellation: Within 2 hours - 10% cancellation fee applies
  • No-Show: If technician arrives and customer is unavailable - no refund
  • How to Cancel: Via app or call our support number

2. Refund Eligibility

Full Refund - When Applicable:

1Technician no-show (after 30 min wait)
2Service cannot be completed due to technical reasons
3Duplicate payment or incorrect charge

Note: Refunds are NOT applicable for: change of mind after service completion, partial use of service, or customer not available at location.

3. Refund Processing Time

  • Initiated: Within 24 hours of refund approval

4. AMC Plan Refunds

  • Annual plans can be cancelled within 7 days of purchase for full refund
  • After 7 days, pro-rata refund based on remaining months
  • No refund for services already availed under the plan
  • Admin fee of ₹100 may apply for processing

5. Product Returns (CCTV/Equipment)

  • Returns accepted within 7 days of installation if product is defective
  • Product must be in original packaging with all accessories
  • Installation removal charges may apply
  • Product inspection required before refund approval
