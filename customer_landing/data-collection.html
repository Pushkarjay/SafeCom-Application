Data Collection Disclosure

Transparency in how we handle your data

Compliance: This disclosure is prepared in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) of India. SafeCom is committed to lawful, fair, and transparent data processing.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only the data necessary to provide our services:

Data TypePurposeBasis
Phone NumberAccount authentication, OTP verificationConsent
Full NamePersonalization, service recordsConsent
Email AddressReceipts, notifications, supportConsent
AddressTechnician dispatch, service deliveryConsent
Location (Precise)Service booking, route optimizationConsent
Payment DetailsProcessed via Razorpay (not stored by us)Contract
Device InfoApp analytics, troubleshootingLegitimate Interest

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Service Delivery: Connect you with technicians, schedule appointments
  • Payments: Process transactions securely through Razorpay
  • Communication: Send booking confirmations, receipts, updates
  • Support: Respond to your queries and resolve issues
  • Improvement: Analyze usage patterns to enhance our services
  • Legal: Comply with tax and regulatory requirements

3. Data Sharing

  • Technicians: Name, address, service details for job completion
  • Razorpay: Payment processing (PCI-DSS compliant)
  • Legal: When required by law or court order

We do NOT sell your data to any third party.

4. Data Retention

  • Account Data: Retained until account deletion
  • Booking History: 2 years for warranty purposes
  • Payment Records: 6 years (as per tax laws)
  • Location Data: Deleted after service completion

5. Your Rights

Under DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • Know: What data we collect and why
  • Access: Get a copy of your personal data
  • Correct: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Delete: Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out: Withdraw consent for location tracking anytime
  • Grievance: File complaint with our Data Protection Officer

6. Security Measures

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for data in transit
  • Firebase security rules for data at rest
  • Role-based access controls
  • Regular security audits

7. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase: Authentication, database, hosting (Google)
  • Razorpay: Payment processing
  • Google Analytics: App usage analytics
